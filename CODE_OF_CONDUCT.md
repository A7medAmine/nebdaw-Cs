# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want nebdaw to be a welcoming place for everyone, especially students taking their first steps in programming and open source. We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, language, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be patient and kind with beginners. There are no stupid questions here.
- Give feedback on the code, not the person.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Respect different opinions, languages and levels of experience.
- Assume good intentions.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, mockery or belittling someone's skill level.
- Harassment, in public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Discriminatory jokes or comments.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a classroom.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or see unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately through their GitHub profile ([@A7medAmine](https://github.com/A7medAmine)). Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code. Depending on how serious it is, they may give a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban from the project.
